Ticket & refund terms

Tickets are non-refundable for buyer cancellations. If the organizer cancels the event, buyers receive a full refund.

Privacy

We use your name, email, order details, and submitted M-Pesa transaction code to verify payment, issue tickets, and support entry. Contact us to request access or correction of your information.
